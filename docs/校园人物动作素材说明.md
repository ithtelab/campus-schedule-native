+## boy

Use case: game-assets / native UI character animation sprite sheet. Create a polished production animation ATLAS, not a poster: exactly 16 isolated frames in a perfectly regular 4 COLUMN × 4 ROW grid, read left-to-right then top-to-bottom. Portrait canvas 1024×2048 or larger SAME 1:2 aspect; every cell is identical portrait proportions, 256×512 at 1024×2048. NO text, NO labels, NO drawn grid lines. Outside all art is genuinely transparent. Each frame shows the SAME university student at the SAME pixel scale with fixed camera, fixed ground/foot baseline at 92% of cell height, character horizontally centered, entire head/hands/feet kept inside its cell with at least 12% clear side margins. No anatomy or costume changes between frames.

Reference image 1 supplies the exact black-and-white campus hand-drawn visual style and student identity. Keep natural BLACK hair, BLACK details, WHITE skin and clothing areas. Add only a fine crisp WHITE outer keyline outside black silhouette areas so the SAME atlas can be read on black or white UI backgrounds. Never invert colors. No gray shading, no gradients, no glow, no shadows. Rounded thick slightly irregular ink contours, simple dot eye and minimal face, original cute college streetwear style. Solid white parts must remain opaque. The background outside figures must remain transparent.

A tiny simple stool may be present to support sitting: if used, keep it IDENTICAL at exactly the same location behind the student in ALL 16 frames. No detached chair fragments, floor lines, horizon baselines, dots or decorative strokes beyond the figure's cell. The student walks in place near the stool; the camera and stool never move. A closed book rests visibly beside the stool when it is not in the student's hands. Use consistent body proportions, line weight and fixed registration throughout.

Exact frame storyboard (row-major):

1: SAME seated pose, hands halfway closing the book.

6: fully STANDING, hands empty, natural relaxed arms.
7: standing, leaning/reaching down toward the closed book.
8: standing upright, CLOSED book held at waist/chest.
9: standing, book OPEN, eyes down reading.
10: walking in place, LEFT foot forward, hands empty, natural arm swing.
11: walking passing position, legs near center, opposite arms.
12: walking in place, RIGHT foot forward, opposite arm swing.
13: opposite walking passing position, legs near center; must form a coherent 10→11→12→13→10 loop.
14: halfway sitting back onto the stool, empty hands lowering, knees bent.

Critical: the motion must change actual limb, torso and book positions. NOT 16 copies of a static pose with shifted position. All 16 cells must be present and independently usable. Do not add unrelated people or props.
